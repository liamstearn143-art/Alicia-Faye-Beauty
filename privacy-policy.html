Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4th May 2025

This Privacy Policy outlines how Alicia Faye Beauty collects, uses, and protects any personal information you provide when using this website.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Name and contact details (e.g. phone number, email address)
  • Information submitted through contact or booking forms
  • Preferences and details relevant to your appointments or treatments
  • Website usage data through cookies or analytics tools

2. How We Use Your Information

We collect this information to:

  • Respond to enquiries or appointment requests
  • Manage bookings and provide services
  • Personalise your experience and improve the website
  • Comply with any legal obligations

3. Data Storage and Protection

We are committed to keeping your information secure. We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data from unauthorised access, disclosure, or loss.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, distribute, or lease your personal information to third parties unless required by law or with your explicit consent. If we use third-party tools (e.g. booking systems), these are GDPR-compliant and only used to support our services.

5. Your Data Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your information
  • Withdraw consent for future contact at any time

To make a request, please contact me at aliciafaye_beauty@hotmail.com.

6. Cookies

This website may use cookies to improve your browsing experience. You can modify your browser settings to decline cookies if you prefer.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with a revised date.
